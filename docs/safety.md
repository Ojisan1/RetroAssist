# Safety and Responsibility

RetroAssist is a diagnostic *assistant*, not a replacement for skill, caution, or primary documentation.

## Core Principles

- **You remain fully responsible** for every action taken while using this tool.
- The system can (and will) make mistakes, miss context, or give incomplete advice.
- Suggestions are generated from a combination of live observation, retrieved documentation, and general electronics knowledge. None of these sources are guaranteed to be correct or complete for your specific situation.

## High-Risk Work

Extra caution is required for:

- High-voltage power supplies and CRT displays
- Capacitor discharge procedures
- Work involving mains voltage
- Any procedure that could result in personal injury, fire, or permanent damage to rare hardware

For these tasks, treat RetroAssist output as a secondary reference only. Always verify critical steps against original service manuals and established safe practices.

## Recommended Practice

1. Start with a clear understanding of the known fault and a careful visual inspection.
2. Use the tool’s suggestions as a guide for what to check next.
3. Confirm expected voltages, continuity readings, and component values against primary documentation whenever possible.
4. When in doubt, stop and consult additional sources or a more experienced technician.

The goal of RetroAssist is to help competent electronics hobbyists take on unfamiliar classic hardware with greater confidence—not to remove the need for judgment, care, and verification.
