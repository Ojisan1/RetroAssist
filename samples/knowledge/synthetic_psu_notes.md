## No-power first checks

When a vintage power supply board has no output, start with a visual fuse inspection
and continuity check across the mains fuse. Confirm the unit is unplugged and capacitors
are discharged before probing.

Expected: fuse continuity near 0 ohms when healthy. An open fuse often accompanies
other faults; replace only with the correct rating and continue diagnosis upstream.

## Safety

Mains voltage can be lethal. Verify isolation and follow primary documentation.
